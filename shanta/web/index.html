Safety & legal rules

  1. Travelers never carry a closed package. Every item is opened and checked before it goes in the bag.
  2. "Buy it for me" is the safest mode: the traveler buys a new item in a shop, keeps the invoice, and declares it as their purchase.
  3. Payment is held by a licensed payment provider and released only when the recipient gives the 6-digit delivery code.
  4. Everyone verifies their ID. Travelers and senders rate each other after delivery.
  5. The traveler is legally responsible for what is in their bag at customs. Declare anything above the duty-free allowance.
